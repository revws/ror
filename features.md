## 1. System Architecture & Elevated Execution
- **Dual-Tier Elevation Engine**:
  - **Full Root Mode**: Direct su shell bridge utilizing modern `libsu` IPC daemon for Magisk, KernelSU, and APatch.
  - **Rootless Shizuku Mode**: Deep integration with the Shizuku IPC framework (`dev.rikka.shizuku:api`), executing system-level operations on unrooted devices.
  - **Automated Silent Permissions**: Automatically provisions `DUMP`, `PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`, `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, `WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS`, and `ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY` silently without interrupting the user.
- **Hardware Acceleration**: Full hardware rendering acceleration across all consoles, charts, and custom Canvas views (`android:hardwareAccelerated="true"`).
- **100% Telemetry-Free Privacy Core**: Strict zero-telemetry architecture. Absolutely no ad networks, no analytics trackers, and no background network pings.
